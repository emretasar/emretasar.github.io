This site is a compact home for the things I learn while building software.

My work is rooted in C++ and Qt/QML. Small decisions in these systems can have real consequences for performance, reliability, and maintainability. I will use this space to write down useful patterns, explain trade-offs, and keep a record of problems worth remembering.

## What to expect

Expect practical notes on modern C++, Qt/QML, communication protocols, UAV ground-control development, and the backend topics I am exploring. The goal is clarity, not volume.

I hope these notes are useful to others—and a useful reference for my future self.
